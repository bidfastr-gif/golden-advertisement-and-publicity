Scaling a Retail Hub from 0 to 29K Followers Through Strategic Community Marketing

Uptown Square Kathipara needed to establish itself as a recognized retail and lifestyle destination near one of Chennai’s busiest junctions. With zero digital presence, the opportunity was to build awareness and drive footfall through social media.

The Challenge

Create a vibrant online community while improving visibility for retail outlets and commercial tenants.

Our Strategy

We executed a structured retail marketing plan that included:

  • Store highlight campaigns
  • Event coverage and promotional reels
  • Influencer led exposure
  • Lifestyle and weekend engagement content
  • Consistent branding and location-based positioning

The focus was clear, transforming Uptown Square from just a commercial property into a digitally active lifestyle hub.

The Results

Instagram growth from 0 to 29K followers

Strong online community engagement

Increased visibility for tenant brands

Improved brand recognition within the Kathipara retail zone
