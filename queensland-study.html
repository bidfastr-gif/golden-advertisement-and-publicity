From Zero Presence to 18K Followers – Building a Leading Entertainment Brand Online

When Queensland Amusement Park partnered with GAP, the brand had no digital footprint on Instagram. In a competitive entertainment market like Chennai, visibility is everything - especially for an amusement park targeting families, students, and corporate groups.

The Challenge

Establish a strong social media presence from scratch and position Queensland as a must-visit weekend and holiday destination.

Our Strategy

We implemented a high-impact content strategy centered around:

  • Reel-first content marketing
  • Festival and seasonal campaign promotions
  • Ride experience highlights and visitor reactions
  • Community driven engagement content
  • Trend-based short-form videos to maximize reach

We focused heavily on visual storytelling, showcasing thrill rides, cable car experiences, happy family moments, and cultural celebrations to emotionally connect with Chennai audiences.

The Results

Instagram growth from 0 to 17.9K followers

Strong brand recall within the Chennai entertainment segment

Increased digital engagement during peak seasons

Enhanced event awareness and weekend traction
